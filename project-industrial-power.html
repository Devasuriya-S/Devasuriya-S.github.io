Project 01 / Personal learning project

Industrial Power
Distribution Design

Project Description

This personal learning project focused on designing a robust and efficient industrial power distribution system for a medium-scale facility. The design process covered the complete flow from understanding the electrical demand and operational requirements to developing a reliable distribution network that supports safety, selectivity and maintainability.

Starting with the overview of this project - This is an industrial furnace plant which starts by transporting products(metals) to the furnace. Inside the furnace the metals are heated at required temperature and then transferred to quenching conveyor. In quenching conveyor the products undergo quenching for 90 minutes. Then the products are moved to output conveyor where they are weighed and packed into boxes.

The work included preparing single-line diagrams and control schematics, assessing connected loads and diversity, and selecting suitable switchgear, protective devices, transformers, busbars and cables. Cable routing, voltage drop and protection coordination were considered as part of the design. ETAP was used to perform load-flow and short-circuit studies and to assess system behaviour under normal and fault conditions.

The instrumenation side of this project has been vast as there were lot of equipments. Starting with sensors to detect the oil level, measuring temperature, load cells for weighing, timers and more. Basics of hydraulics has also been covered in this project.

While the project is still in development phase, 40% of the work has already been completed. Since being a personal project, I am working on this whenever I have time. If you would love to hear more from me feel free to contact me!
